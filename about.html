About Tirupati Metachems

Built on Quality,
Reliability & Technical Expertise.

Tirupati Metachems Private Limited is an Indian manufacturer specialising in resin-coated sands, foundry fluxes, chemicals and related industrial materials. The company has built its reputation around quality, reliability and responsive customer service.

The company supplies to foundry and metal industries across India, helping customers produce cleaner, more consistent castings through practical expertise in molten metal treatment and flux technology.
