Resin Coated Sand

Resin Coated Sand.
Grade-by-Grade.

High-grade silica sand (Cochin / Mangalore / quartz based) coated with phenol-formaldehyde resin and catalyst for the shell process. The sand is smooth, free-flowing and dry with good resistance to moisture — used for cores and moulds in shell moulding across grey iron, malleable iron, S.G. iron, steel, stainless steel and non-ferrous foundries.

Shell Sand Advantages

  • Extremely free-flowing, producing cores and moulds of greater density and less prone to erosion by metal.
  • Hollow cores and moulds are significantly lighter for handling, with the lowest-cost casting production.
  • Deep-drawn patterns with tapers of 0.5° to 3° can be produced by the shell process.
  • High out-of-box strengths yield precise dimensional tolerances, reducing machining, fettling and finishing costs.
  • The shell process eliminates the need for refractory coatings.
  • Cores and moulds remain usable indefinitely — unaffected by humidity and resistant to moisture pickup.
  • Exceptionally resistant to damage during handling and storing.
  • Excellent casting time after time; simple to process, operable with unskilled labour.
  • Low capital investment for core and mould-making equipment, with less sand handling and consumption.

Packing

Resin Coated Shell Sand is supplied in 50 kg laminated HDPE / PP bags. Printed matter includes Date of Manufacture, Batch No. & Bag No.
